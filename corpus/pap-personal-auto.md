# Personal Auto Policy

Cobalt Harbor Mutual Insurance Company (fictional). This form is illustrative and was written for a portfolio project. It is not a real insurance contract.

## 1. Definitions

### 1.1 "Your covered auto"
"Your covered auto" means any vehicle shown in the Declarations; a newly acquired auto; any trailer you own; and any auto or trailer you do not own while used as a temporary substitute for a covered auto that is out of normal use because of breakdown, repair, servicing, loss or destruction.

### 1.2 Newly acquired auto
A newly acquired auto is one you become the owner of during the policy period. If it replaces a vehicle shown in the Declarations, it has the same coverage as the vehicle it replaced, and you must ask us to insure it within 14 days only if you wish to add or continue Physical Damage coverage. If it is an additional vehicle, coverage begins on the date you become the owner, but you must ask us to insure it within 14 days after you become the owner.

### 1.3 "Family member"
"Family member" means a person related to you by blood, marriage or adoption who is a resident of your household, including a ward or foster child.

## 2. Part A - Liability Coverage

### 2.1 Insuring agreement
We will pay damages for bodily injury or property damage for which any insured becomes legally responsible because of an auto accident. We will settle or defend, as we consider appropriate, any claim or suit asking for these damages. In addition to our limit of liability, we will pay all defense costs we incur. Our duty to settle or defend ends when our limit of liability for this coverage has been exhausted by payment of judgments or settlements.

### 2.2 Who is an insured
For Part A, "insured" means you or any family member for the ownership, maintenance or use of any auto or trailer, and any person using your covered auto with a reasonable belief that the person is entitled to do so.

### 2.3 Supplementary payments
In addition to our limit of liability, we will pay on behalf of an insured: up to $250 for the cost of bail bonds required because of an accident, including related traffic law violations; premiums on appeal bonds and bonds to release attachments in any suit we defend; up to $200 a day for loss of earnings because of attendance at hearings or trials at our request; and other reasonable expenses incurred at our request.

### 2.4 Liability exclusions
We do not provide Liability Coverage for any insured:
- Who intentionally causes bodily injury or property damage.
- For property damage to property owned or being transported by that insured.
- For that insured's liability arising out of the ownership or operation of a vehicle while it is being used as a public or livery conveyance. This exclusion does not apply to a share-the-expense car pool.
- While employed or otherwise engaged in the business of selling, repairing, servicing, storing or parking vehicles.
- Using a vehicle without a reasonable belief that that insured is entitled to do so.

### 2.5 Limit of liability
The limit of liability shown in the Declarations for each person for Bodily Injury Liability is our maximum limit for all damages for bodily injury sustained by any one person in any one auto accident. Subject to that limit, the limit shown for each accident is our maximum for all bodily injury resulting from any one auto accident. The Property Damage limit is our maximum for all property damage resulting from any one accident. This is the most we will pay regardless of the number of insureds, claims made, or vehicles involved.

## 3. Part B - Medical Payments Coverage

### 3.1 Insuring agreement
We will pay reasonable expenses incurred for necessary medical and funeral services because of bodily injury caused by accident and sustained by an insured. We will pay only those expenses incurred for services rendered within three years from the date of the accident.

### 3.2 Medical payments exclusions
We do not provide Medical Payments Coverage for any insured for bodily injury sustained while occupying any vehicle located for use as a residence or premises, or while occupying your covered auto when it is being used as a public or livery conveyance, or sustained during the course of employment if workers' compensation benefits are required or available.

## 4. Part C - Uninsured Motorists Coverage

### 4.1 Insuring agreement
We will pay compensatory damages which an insured is legally entitled to recover from the owner or operator of an uninsured motor vehicle because of bodily injury sustained by an insured and caused by an accident.

### 4.2 Uninsured motor vehicle
"Uninsured motor vehicle" means a land motor vehicle or trailer to which no bodily injury liability bond or policy applies at the time of the accident; to which a policy applies but its limit is less than the minimum limit required by the financial responsibility law of the state where your covered auto is principally garaged; or which is a hit-and-run vehicle whose operator or owner cannot be identified and which hits you, a family member, or a vehicle you or a family member are occupying. A hit-and-run accident must be reported to police within 24 hours and to us within 30 days.

## 5. Part D - Coverage for Damage to Your Auto

### 5.1 Insuring agreement
We will pay for direct and accidental loss to your covered auto or any non-owned auto, including their equipment, minus any applicable deductible shown in the Declarations. If loss to more than one covered auto results from the same collision, only the highest applicable deductible will apply. We will pay for loss to your covered auto caused by: other than collision, only if the Declarations indicate that Other Than Collision Coverage is provided; and collision, only if the Declarations indicate that Collision Coverage is provided.

### 5.2 Collision and other than collision
"Collision" means the upset of your covered auto or a non-owned auto or their impact with another vehicle or object. Loss caused by the following is considered other than collision (comprehensive): missiles or falling objects; fire; theft or larceny; explosion or earthquake; windstorm; hail, water or flood; malicious mischief or vandalism; riot or civil commotion; contact with bird or animal; or breakage of glass. If breakage of glass is caused by a collision, you may elect to have it considered a loss caused by collision.

### 5.3 Transportation expenses
In addition to our limit of liability, we will pay, without application of a deductible, up to $20 per day, to a maximum of $600, for temporary transportation expenses incurred by you because of the total theft of your covered auto. We will pay only expenses incurred during the period beginning 48 hours after the theft and ending when your covered auto is returned or we pay for its loss. If a Rental Reimbursement endorsement is shown in the Declarations, its daily and maximum limits apply instead.

### 5.4 Physical damage exclusions
We will not pay for:
- Loss to your covered auto which occurs while it is being used as a public or livery conveyance.
- Damage due and confined to wear and tear, freezing, mechanical or electrical breakdown or failure, or road damage to tires.
- Loss due to or as a consequence of discharge of any nuclear weapon, war, insurrection or rebellion.
- Loss to any electronic equipment not permanently installed in your covered auto.
- Loss to your covered auto or any non-owned auto while used in any organized racing or speed contest or in practice or preparation for such a contest.
- Loss to a non-owned auto when used by you or any family member without a reasonable belief that you or that family member are entitled to do so.

### 5.5 Limit of liability and payment of loss
Our limit of liability for loss will be the lesser of the actual cash value of the stolen or damaged property, or the amount necessary to repair or replace the property with other property of like kind and quality. An adjustment for depreciation and physical condition will be made in determining actual cash value in the event of a total loss. We may pay for loss in money or repair or replace the damaged or stolen property.

## 6. Part E - Duties After an Accident or Loss

### 6.1 General duties
We have no duty to provide coverage under this policy unless there has been full compliance with the following duties. We must be notified promptly of how, when and where the accident or loss happened, including the names and addresses of any injured persons and of any witnesses. A person seeking any coverage must cooperate with us in the investigation, settlement or defense of any claim or suit, and promptly send us copies of any notices or legal papers received in connection with the accident or loss.

### 6.2 Additional duties for physical damage
A person seeking coverage under Part D must also: take reasonable steps after loss to protect your covered auto or any non-owned auto and their equipment from further loss, and we will pay reasonable expenses incurred to do this; promptly notify the police if your covered auto or any non-owned auto is stolen; and permit us to inspect and appraise the damaged property before its repair or disposal.

## 7. Part F - General Provisions

### 7.1 Transportation network and delivery use
This policy does not provide any coverage while your covered auto is being used to transport passengers or deliver goods for compensation through a transportation network company or delivery platform, from the time the driver logs on to the platform until the time the driver logs off, unless a Ride-Share and Delivery endorsement is shown in the Declarations.

### 7.2 Policy territory
This policy applies only to accidents and losses which occur within the United States of America, its territories or possessions, Puerto Rico, or Canada, or while your covered auto is being transported between their ports.

### 7.3 Cancellation by us
We may cancel this policy by mailing notice to the named insured. If the policy has been in effect less than 60 days, we will give at least 10 days notice. After a policy has been in effect for 60 days, we will cancel only for nonpayment of premium, or if your driver's license or that of any driver who lives with you or customarily uses your covered auto has been suspended or revoked, or if the policy was obtained through material misrepresentation. Notice for nonpayment is at least 10 days; for any other reason, at least 20 days.
