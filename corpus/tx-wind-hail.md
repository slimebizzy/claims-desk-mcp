# Texas Windstorm and Hail Deductible Endorsement

Cobalt Harbor Mutual Insurance Company (fictional). This endorsement is illustrative and was written for a portfolio project. It does not state Texas law. It modifies the Homeowners Policy Form HO-3 for residence premises located in Texas.

## 1. Windstorm or Hail Deductible

### 1.1 Percentage deductible
For loss caused by windstorm or hail, the deductible in HO-3 Section 6.1 is replaced by a deductible equal to the percentage of the Coverage A limit shown in the Declarations for this endorsement. The available percentages are 1%, 2%, and 5%. The windstorm or hail deductible will not be less than $1,000.

### 1.2 Example
If the Coverage A limit is $300,000 and the Declarations show a 2% windstorm or hail deductible, the deductible for a hail loss is $6,000. We will pay only that part of the loss that exceeds $6,000.

### 1.3 Application to all coverages
The windstorm or hail deductible applies to the total of all loss payable under Coverages A, B and C combined that results from a single windstorm or hail event. All windstorm or hail damage occurring within any 72-hour period is considered a single event.

## 2. Cosmetic Roof Damage

### 2.1 Cosmetic damage exclusion
We do not cover cosmetic damage to roof surfacing caused by hail. Cosmetic damage means marring, pitting or denting that changes the appearance of the roof surface but does not prevent the roof from continuing to function as a barrier to water entering the dwelling, and does not shorten the expected life of the roof.

### 2.2 Roof surfacing settlement
Loss to roof surfacing caused by windstorm or hail is settled at actual cash value, not replacement cost, if the roof surfacing is more than 15 years old at the time of loss. Roof surfacing 15 years old or less is settled under HO-3 Section 6.3.

## 3. Claims Reporting

### 3.1 Reporting deadline
A claim for windstorm or hail damage must be reported to us within one year after the date of the windstorm or hail event, unless good cause exists for later reporting.

### 3.2 Inspection
We will inspect the damage within 15 days after receiving notice of the claim, unless a catastrophe has been declared, in which case the inspection period may be extended by 15 days.
