# California Homeowners Amendatory Endorsement

Cobalt Harbor Mutual Insurance Company (fictional). This endorsement is illustrative and was written for a portfolio project. It does not state California law. It modifies the Homeowners Policy Form HO-3 for residence premises located in California. Where this endorsement conflicts with the HO-3 form, this endorsement controls.

## 1. Additional Living Expense - Declared State of Emergency

### 1.1 Extended time limit
HO-3 Section 2.5 (Coverage D - Loss of Use) is amended. If a covered loss relates to a state of emergency declared by the Governor, Additional Living Expense is payable for a period of up to 24 months instead of 12 months. We will grant an extension of up to 12 additional months, for a total of 36 months, if you act in good faith and with reasonable diligence and encounter delays in reconstruction beyond your control.

### 1.2 Advance payment
If a covered loss relating to a declared state of emergency makes the residence premises uninhabitable, we will provide an advance payment of no less than four months of Additional Living Expense upon request.

### 1.3 Civil authority
The two-week civil authority limit in HO-3 Section 2.5 is replaced with a limit of four weeks for California residence premises.

## 2. Wildfire and Total Loss

### 2.1 Contents advance
In the event of a covered total loss of the dwelling relating to a declared state of emergency, we will offer a payment under Coverage C of no less than 30% of the Coverage C limit, up to $250,000, without requiring an itemized inventory.

### 2.2 Rebuilding at another location
If you choose to rebuild or replace the dwelling at a location other than the residence premises, we will pay the replacement cost of the dwelling, up to the Coverage A limit, measured as the cost to rebuild at the original residence premises. The value of the land at the new location is not included.

### 2.3 Time to collect replacement cost
For a total loss relating to a declared state of emergency, the 180-day period in HO-3 Section 6.3 to make a claim for additional replacement cost is replaced with a period of no less than 36 months from the date of the first actual cash value payment.

## 3. Conditions

### 3.1 Suit against us
HO-3 Section 6.6 is amended: the two-year period to bring an action is tolled from the time you give notice of the claim until we formally deny the claim in writing.

### 3.2 Earthquake
Earthquake remains excluded under HO-3 Section 5.1. California residents may purchase earthquake coverage through a separate earthquake policy. We will offer earthquake coverage at least every other renewal.
