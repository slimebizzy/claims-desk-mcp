# Homeowners Policy Form HO-3 (Special Form)

Cobalt Harbor Mutual Insurance Company (fictional). This form is illustrative and was written for a portfolio project. It is not a real insurance contract.

## 1. Definitions

### 1.1 "You" and "your"
"You" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the Declarations and the spouse or domestic partner of the named insured if a resident of the same household.

### 1.2 "Residence premises"
"Residence premises" means the one-family dwelling where you reside, including the grounds and other structures, shown as the residence premises in the Declarations.

### 1.3 "Actual cash value"
"Actual cash value" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with property of like kind and quality, less a deduction for depreciation, including physical deterioration and obsolescence.

### 1.4 "Replacement cost"
"Replacement cost" means the cost to repair or replace damaged property with material of like kind and quality, for the same use, without deduction for depreciation.

## 2. Property Coverages

### 2.1 Coverage A - Dwelling
We cover the dwelling on the residence premises, including structures attached to the dwelling, and materials and supplies on or next to the residence premises used to construct, alter or repair the dwelling.

### 2.2 Coverage B - Other Structures
We cover other structures on the residence premises set apart from the dwelling by clear space, such as a detached garage, fence or shed. The limit for Coverage B is 10% of the Coverage A limit unless a different limit is shown in the Declarations. We do not cover other structures rented to any person who is not a tenant of the dwelling, unless used solely as a private garage.

### 2.3 Coverage C - Personal Property
We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world. The limit for Coverage C is 50% of the Coverage A limit unless a different limit is shown in the Declarations. Personal property usually located at an insured's residence other than the residence premises is limited to 10% of the Coverage C limit or $1,000, whichever is greater.

### 2.4 Special Limits of Liability for Coverage C
The following special limits apply to each loss for all property in each category:
- $200 on money, bank notes, gold other than goldware, and coins.
- $1,500 on securities, accounts, deeds, manuscripts, passports and tickets.
- $1,500 on watercraft, including their trailers and outboard engines.
- $1,500 for loss by theft of jewelry, watches, furs, and precious and semiprecious stones.
- $2,500 for loss by theft of firearms and related equipment.
- $2,500 for loss by theft of silverware and goldware.
- $2,500 on property used primarily for business purposes while on the residence premises.
Higher limits for scheduled items are available by endorsement.

### 2.5 Coverage D - Loss of Use (Additional Living Expense)
If a loss covered under this Section makes the residence premises not fit to live in, we cover Additional Living Expense: any necessary increase in living expenses incurred by you so that your household can maintain its normal standard of living. Payment will be for the shortest time required to repair or replace the damage or, if you permanently relocate, the shortest time for your household to settle elsewhere, but not to exceed 12 months. The limit for Coverage D is 30% of the Coverage A limit. If a civil authority prohibits you from using the residence premises because of direct damage to neighboring premises by a covered peril, we cover Additional Living Expense for no more than two weeks.

## 3. Additional Coverages

### 3.1 Debris Removal
We will pay your reasonable expense for the removal of debris of covered property if a covered peril causes the loss. This expense is included in the limit of liability that applies to the damaged property. If the amount to be paid for the actual damage plus debris removal exceeds the limit, an additional 5% of that limit is available for debris removal. We will also pay up to $1,000 in total for the removal of trees felled by windstorm, hail or weight of ice, snow or sleet, with no more than $500 for any one tree, if the tree damages a covered structure or blocks a driveway.

### 3.2 Reasonable Repairs
We will pay the reasonable cost incurred by you for necessary measures taken solely to protect covered property that is damaged by a covered peril from further damage, such as tarping a damaged roof or boarding broken windows. Keep receipts for these expenses.

### 3.3 Trees, Shrubs and Other Plants
We cover trees, shrubs, plants or lawns on the residence premises for loss caused by fire, lightning, explosion, riot, aircraft, vehicles not owned by a resident, vandalism or theft. We will pay up to 5% of the Coverage A limit for all trees, shrubs, plants or lawns, with no more than $500 for any one tree, shrub or plant. We do not cover loss caused by windstorm to trees, shrubs or plants.

### 3.4 Fungi, Wet or Dry Rot, or Bacteria
We will pay up to $10,000 in total for direct physical loss caused by fungi, mold, wet or dry rot, or bacteria, including the cost to remove it and to tear out and replace covered property to gain access, but only when the fungi or rot results from a covered peril, such as a sudden pipe burst. This is the most we will pay regardless of the number of locations or claims during the policy period.

## 4. Perils Insured Against

### 4.1 Coverage A and B - Open Perils
We insure against direct physical loss to property described in Coverages A and B unless the loss is excluded in Section 5 (Exclusions) or is one of the following:
- Wear and tear, marring, deterioration, mechanical breakdown, latent defect, or inherent vice.
- Smog, rust or other corrosion.
- Settling, shrinking, bulging or expansion, including resulting cracking of foundations, walls, floors or ceilings.
- Birds, rodents, insects, or animals owned or kept by an insured.
- Continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water or steam over a period of 14 or more days from within a plumbing, heating or air conditioning system or household appliance.
- Freezing of a plumbing system while the dwelling is vacant or unoccupied, unless you used reasonable care to maintain heat or shut off the water supply and drain the system.
If any of these cause water damage not otherwise excluded, we cover the resulting water damage, including the cost of tearing out and replacing part of the building to access the system, but not the system or appliance itself.

### 4.2 Coverage C - Named Perils
We insure Coverage C personal property for direct physical loss caused only by the following named perils: fire or lightning; windstorm or hail; explosion; riot or civil commotion; aircraft; vehicles; smoke; vandalism or malicious mischief; theft; falling objects; weight of ice, snow or sleet; accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam; sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning or bulging of a heating or air conditioning system or water heater; freezing; sudden and accidental damage from artificially generated electrical current; and volcanic eruption.

## 5. Exclusions

### 5.1 Earth Movement
We do not cover loss caused by earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mudflow, sinkhole, or subsidence, regardless of any other cause contributing to the loss. However, we do cover direct loss by fire or explosion that results from earth movement. Earthquake coverage may be purchased separately by endorsement.

### 5.2 Water
We do not cover loss caused by: flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, or overflow of a body of water, whether or not driven by wind; water or water-borne material that backs up through sewers or drains or overflows from a sump pump; or water below the surface of the ground that seeps through a foundation, basement floor or wall. Flood coverage is available only through a separate flood policy. Water backup coverage is available by endorsement with a sublimit.

### 5.3 Neglect and Intentional Loss
We do not cover loss caused by neglect, meaning failure of an insured to use all reasonable means to save and preserve property at and after the time of a loss. We do not cover any loss arising out of any act an insured commits with the intent to cause a loss.

### 5.4 Ordinance or Law
We do not cover the increased cost of construction required by any ordinance or law regulating the construction, repair or demolition of a building, except as provided by an Ordinance or Law endorsement if shown in the Declarations.

### 5.5 Power Failure and War
We do not cover loss caused by failure of power or other utility service if the failure takes place off the residence premises, unless a covered peril ensues on the residence premises. We do not cover loss caused by war, including undeclared war, or nuclear hazard.

## 6. Section I Conditions

### 6.1 Deductible
Unless otherwise noted in this policy, the following deductible provision applies: we will pay only that part of the total of all loss payable that exceeds the deductible amount shown in the Declarations. A separate windstorm or hail deductible may apply if shown by endorsement.

### 6.2 Duties After Loss
In case of a loss to covered property, you must see that the following are done:
- Give prompt notice to us or our agent.
- Notify the police in case of loss by theft.
- Protect the property from further damage and make reasonable and necessary repairs to protect it.
- Prepare an inventory of damaged personal property showing the quantity, description, actual cash value and amount of loss, with bills, receipts and related documents.
- As often as we reasonably require, show the damaged property and submit to examination under oath.
- Send to us, within 60 days after our request, your signed, sworn proof of loss.

### 6.3 Loss Settlement - Buildings
Buildings under Coverage A or B are settled at replacement cost without deduction for depreciation, subject to the following: if, at the time of loss, the amount of insurance on the damaged building is 80% or more of its full replacement cost, we will pay the cost to repair or replace, up to the limit of liability. If it is less than 80%, we will pay the greater of the actual cash value of the damaged part or that proportion of the cost to repair or replace that the limit of insurance bears to 80% of the replacement cost. We will pay no more than actual cash value until actual repair or replacement is complete. You may make a claim on an actual cash value basis and then make a claim for any additional replacement cost within 180 days after the loss.

### 6.4 Loss Settlement - Personal Property
Personal property, awnings, carpeting, household appliances and outdoor antennas are settled at actual cash value at the time of loss but not more than the amount required to repair or replace, unless a Personal Property Replacement Cost endorsement is shown in the Declarations.

### 6.5 Appraisal
If you and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either may demand an appraisal of the loss. Each party will choose a competent and impartial appraiser within 20 days after receiving a written request from the other. The two appraisers will choose an umpire. A decision agreed to by any two will set the amount of loss. Each party pays its own appraiser and shares equally the expense of the umpire.

### 6.6 Suit Against Us
No action can be brought against us unless there has been full compliance with all of the terms under this Section and the action is started within two years after the date of loss.

### 6.7 Loss Payment
We will adjust all losses with you. We will pay you unless some other person is named in the policy or is legally entitled to receive payment. Loss will be payable 60 days after we receive your proof of loss and an agreement is reached, a final judgment is entered, or an appraisal award is filed with us.

## 7. Section II - Liability Coverages

### 7.1 Coverage E - Personal Liability
If a claim is made or a suit is brought against an insured for damages because of bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence to which this coverage applies, we will pay up to our limit of liability for the damages for which an insured is legally liable, and provide a defense at our expense by counsel of our choice. Our duty to defend ends when our limit of liability has been exhausted by payment of a judgment or settlement.

### 7.2 Coverage F - Medical Payments to Others
We will pay the necessary medical expenses that are incurred or medically ascertained within three years from the date of an accident causing bodily injury, to a person on the insured location with the permission of an insured. This coverage does not apply to you or regular residents of your household. The limit is shown in the Declarations and is typically $1,000 or $5,000 per person.

### 7.3 Section II Exclusions
Coverages E and F do not apply to bodily injury or property damage: which is expected or intended by an insured; arising out of business activities of an insured; arising out of the rental of any part of a premises by an insured; arising out of the ownership, maintenance or use of motor vehicles, except certain golf carts and recreational vehicles while on an insured location; or arising out of the transmission of a communicable disease by an insured. Coverage E does not apply to bodily injury to you or an insured.
