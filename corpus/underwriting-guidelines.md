# Homeowners Underwriting Guidelines (Internal)

Cobalt Harbor Mutual Insurance Company (fictional). Internal use only. Not for distribution to policyholders or claims staff. Illustrative content written for a portfolio project.

## 1. Eligibility

### 1.1 Roof age
New business is eligible for replacement cost on roof surfacing only if the roof is 20 years old or less. Roofs older than 20 years require an actual cash value roof endorsement or an inspection showing at least 5 years of remaining life.

### 1.2 Prior losses
Decline new business with three or more property losses in the past 5 years, or any single water loss over $25,000 in the past 3 years without documented remediation.

### 1.3 Ineligible risks
The following are ineligible: dwellings in foreclosure; dwellings vacant or unoccupied for more than 60 days; properties with an unfenced swimming pool; properties used for short-term rental more than 30 days per year without a home-sharing endorsement; and dwellings with knob-and-tube wiring.

## 2. Insurance to Value

### 2.1 Replacement cost estimate
Every new business and every third renewal requires a replacement cost estimate using the approved estimator. Coverage A must be at least 100% of the estimated replacement cost at new business. Renewals below 90% receive an automatic inflation adjustment.

### 2.2 Coverage C reductions
Coverage C may be reduced to no less than 40% of Coverage A at the applicant's request.

## 3. Referral Authority

### 3.1 Referral thresholds
Refer to a senior underwriter any risk with Coverage A above $1,500,000, any dwelling built before 1940, or any wildfire risk score of 8 or higher on the 10-point scale. Underwriters may not bind wildfire risk scores of 9 or 10 without Regional Underwriting Manager approval.
